Knapsack: Greedy Strategies Compared

We compare four greedy approaches to the 0/1 knapsack problem (plus the fractional relaxation). Each strategy sorts items by a different criterion and greedily packs them into the knapsack.

Problem Instance

Item Weight Value Ratio (v/w)
0 100 40 0.40
1 50 35 0.70
2 45 18 0.40
3 20 4 0.20
4 10 10 1.00
5 5 2 0.40

Capacity W = 100. The optimal 0/1 solution has value 55.

Strategy Results

Each strategy sorts items by a different criterion, then greedily packs them if they fit.

Max Value Strategy

Sort by decreasing value. Pick the most valuable items first.

Min Weight Strategy

Sort by increasing weight. Pick the lightest items first.

Best Ratio Strategy

Sort by decreasing value-to-weight ratio. This is the most effective greedy heuristic for 0/1 knapsack.

Fractional Relaxation

Allow partial items. Sort by ratio and take fractions when an item does not fully fit. This gives an upper bound on the 0/1 optimum.

Strategy Comparison

Key takeaway: The Best Ratio greedy heuristic achieves the optimum on this instance, while the Fractional relaxation provides an upper bound. Max Value and Min Weight perform worse because they do not balance weight and value.
