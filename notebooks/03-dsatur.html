DSatur: Graph Colouring by Saturation Degree

DSatur (Degree of Saturation) is a greedy graph-colouring heuristic. At each step it picks the uncoloured vertex with the highest saturation degree (the number of distinct colours among its already-coloured neighbours), breaking ties by highest uncoloured degree, then alphabetically. It assigns the smallest available colour.

We compare DSatur against a simpler Max Degree ordering (static ordering by vertex degree).
